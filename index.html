How to Play

The goal

Explore an endless city from your home, drive any car you like, finish missions for cash, or cause trouble and escape the police. There is no wrong way to play.

Walking and driving

Move
Phone: Gas walks or drives forward, Brake slows down and reverses, ◀ ▶ turn. Keyboard: W S A D or the arrow keys.
Get in / out
Walk up to a vehicle and tap Enter (or press E). Tap Exit to get out. Stop first, you cannot jump out at speed.
Nitro
Hold NOS (or Shift) while accelerating. The blue bar under the speedometer refills on its own.
Handbrake
Space for sharp turns and drifts.
Run
On foot, hold Shift to sprint.
Camera
Tap Cam (or press C) to switch between close and far views.
Vehicle health
The yellow bar under the speedometer. Big crashes and gunfire wear it down. At zero the vehicle explodes, so get out early if it starts smoking.

Calling a vehicle

Open the Garage tab to see every vehicle and its number, such as 1101 for the Toyota Fortuner or 1301 for the Lamborghini Huracán. The fastest is the Koenigsegg Jesko (1306): 1990 km/h, over 2100 km/h on nitro. It is bulletproof and can never be damaged or blown up, and nothing can hurt you while you sit in it. Tap a vehicle, or type its number in the Dialer and press Call. It drops in right beside you. You can have up to 8 called vehicles at once.

Tesla Roadster (flying car)

Call 1307. On the ground it reaches 10,000 km/h. It never takes damage, never explodes, and nothing can hurt you inside it.

FLY
Tap FLY (or press G) to open the map. Put your finger on your car and drag a green dotted line to any spot, then let go. The Roadster lifts off on its thrusters, flies there at 2,000 km/h and lands on that exact spot. Drop the line on a building to land on its roof.
CLIMB
Drive right up to a building wall and CLIMB appears (or press V). The car drives straight up the wall and onto the roof. You can drive around the roof, then use FLY to come down.

Dubai life

Clock and prayers
The clock at the top right runs fast: a whole day takes 16 minutes. When Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib or Isha begins you get a message and the clock turns green. Walk to a masjid (the clock shows how far) and press E at the door.
Masjid
Inside, go to the wudu area first and watch the full ablution, then step onto the carpet and press E to pray with the jamaat. Each prayer has its real number of rak'ahs. You earn XP and a gem.
Food
The orange FOOD bar under your health drops slowly. At zero you start losing health. Eat at any restaurant or the Dubai Mall food court.
Restaurants and the mall
Sit at a table with E, choose from the menu, wait for the waiter, press E to eat. Pay at the counter. If you try to leave without paying, guards catch you, take you to the counter and charge the bill plus a fine.
Hotels
Enter any hotel (or the Burj Al Arab on its island). At reception choose a room type, a floor and how long to stay. Then use the lifts to ride to your floor. In your room you can sleep to restore health, order room service and enjoy the view. When your time ends you are sent back to the lobby.
Clothes and phones
Buy clothes at Fashion Avenue in the Dubai Mall, then open your phone, go to Settings and tap Wear. The Store tab sells phones: better phones bring your vehicle faster.
Level
Missions, prayers, meals and shopping give XP. The bar at the top left shows your level.

Helicopter

Call 6001, or climb into the one on your garage roof. Hold Space to rise, Shift to go down, W to fly forward and A D to turn. You can land on roofs and on the helipad of the Burj Al Arab. Land on the street to get out.

Stealing a car

Stand in front of a car in traffic and it will stop. Walk to its side and tap Enter (or E) to pull the driver out and take it. If the police see you do it, you get a star.

Weapons

Pick a gun
Tap a weapon in the bar at the top of the screen (or press 1-5). Tap it again to put it away. You start with no gun out.
Shoot
Hold FIRE (or left-click / F). Shots aim at the nearest target in front of you. You can also shoot from inside a vehicle.
Zoom
Tap Zoom (or hold right-click / press Z), then drag your finger or move the mouse to aim at the crosshair. With the sniper rifle this opens the scope. Tap Unzoom to stop.
1 Pistol
Accurate, one shot at a time.
2 Machine Gun
Very fast, sprays bullets.
3 Assault Rifle
Fast and stronger, longer range.
4 Sniper Rifle
One shot takes out a person or nearly any car. Best with Zoom.
5 Grenade Launcher
Fires a grenade in an arc that explodes on impact and blows up cars around it.

Police and wanted stars

Shooting a car, shooting a person or shooting the police is a crime. Bumping into people with a car is not: they just jump out of the way. Each crime adds a star above your health bar, up to 5. Five police cars will chase you.

If you stop or get out, officers jump out of their cars and shoot. Each officer goes down after 5 pistol shots. The timer next to the stars shows how long until the police give up, about 2 minutes after your last crime. Keep driving and avoid new crimes to lose them.

Health and WASTED

Your HP bar is at the bottom of the screen and heals slowly when you avoid damage. If it reaches zero you see WASTED, wake up at home, lose your stars and pay a ₹500 hospital bill.

Missions and cash

Open the Missions tab (or press M) and tap Start. Follow the coloured beam of light and the dot on the minimap. Finish before the timer ends for a cash bonus. If a mission needs a special vehicle, it is delivered to you.

Map and home

The city is Dubai. The chip at the top left shows the district you are in. The minimap shows roads, the red Metro line along Sheikh Zayed Road, your home (yellow dot), the Burj Khalifa and Burj Al Arab (white and blue dots at the edge), your target, traffic and police (flashing red and blue). The Home tab takes you home when you are not wanted, and has night mode and sound.
